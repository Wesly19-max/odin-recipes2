Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is one of the most iconic Filipino dishes. It is made by braising chicken in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black peppercorns. The result is a savory, slightly tangy dish typically served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken (about 1 kilogram), cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to Taste

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pot over medium heat.
  2. Add garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Pour in soy sauce and water. Let it simmer for about 10 minutes.
  5. Add vinegar, bay leaves, and peppercorns. Do not stir immediately.
  6. Simmer for 20–30 minutes until the chicken becomes tender.
  7. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
