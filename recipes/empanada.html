Empanada

Description

Filipino Empanadas are savory pastries filled with meat, vegetables, and sometimes raisins or boiled eggs. They are either baked or deep-fried and commonly eaten as snacks.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter or margarine
  • 1/2 cup butter or margarine
  • 250 g ground beef
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup diced potatoes
  • 1/2 cup diced carrots
  • 2 tablespoons raisins
  • 2 tablespoons raisins
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cooking oil for frying

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix flour and butter until crumbly.
  2. Add cold water gradually and knead until dough forms.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut into circles.
  4. In a pan, sauté garlic and onion.
  5. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  6. Add potatoes, carrots, and raisins. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Place filling in the center of each dough circle.
  8. Fold and seal the edges.
  9. Fry in hot oil until golden brown.
  10. Drain excess oil and serve warm.
